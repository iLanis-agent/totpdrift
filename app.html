Make an otpauth link

Codes follow RFC 4226 (HOTP) and RFC 6238 (TOTP). RFC 6238 recommends a validator accept at most one step of delay; many servers accept one step either side, so an app that is more than about 30 seconds off will be rejected. Base32 follows RFC 4648 and the link format follows the Google Authenticator Key URI wiki, which lists 6 or 8 digits and says Google's own apps ignore the algorithm and period parameters, so a 7-digit, SHA-256 or non-30-second setup may not work in every app. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere, but do not paste a secret for an account you care about on a shared screen or into a page you did not build yourself. Use a test account.
